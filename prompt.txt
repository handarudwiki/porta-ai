1. MONETARY_POLICY

Contoh:

“Bank Indonesia mempertahankan BI-Rate di 5,25% dan memberi sinyal kemungkinan penurunan suku bunga pada kuartal berikutnya.”

Event type
MONETARY_POLICY
├── RATE_CHANGE
├── RATE_HOLD
├── RATE_CUT
├── QE_QT
└── CENTRAL_BANK_GUIDANCE
Data yang diekstrak
{
  "central_bank": "Bank Indonesia",
  "policy_rate": "BI-Rate",
  "previous_rate": 5.25,
  "new_rate": 5.25,
  "change_bps": 0,
  "direction": "HOLD",
  "effective_date": "...",
  "decision_date": "...",
  "reason": [
    "inflation",
    "exchange_rate"
  ],
  "guidance": "potential easing",
  "guidance_direction": "DOVISH",
  "confidence": 0.96
}
Logic
Rate naik
    ↓
cost of money ↑
    ↓
borrowing cost ↑
    ↓
potential pressure:
- banks
- property
- consumer
- highly leveraged companies

Tapi jangan langsung bilang “BBCA turun”.

Porta harus:

Event
↓
Economic mechanism
↓
Affected sectors
↓
User exposure

Misalnya:

BI +50 bps
↓
Financial sector sensitivity: HIGH
↓
User financial exposure: 48%
↓
Portfolio relevance: HIGH

  "scope": "export",

Policy
↓
Affected activity
↓
Industry
↓
Companies
↓
Portfolio exposure

Nickel exporters
↓
Mining companies

3. REGULATION

Bedakan dengan fiscal policy.

Fiscal = kebijakan ekonomi pemerintah seperti pajak/subsidi.

Regulation = aturan yang mengubah bagaimana perusahaan/industri boleh beroperasi.

Contoh:

“OJK menerbitkan aturan baru mengenai batas kepemilikan fintech.”

Extraction
{
  "regulator": "OJK",
  "regulation_name": "...",
  "action": "NEW_REGULATION",
  "affected_industry": "fintech",
  "affected_entities": [],
  "requirement": "...",
  "threshold": "...",
  "effective_date": "...",
  "transition_period": "...",
  "penalty": "...",
  "status": "ANNOUNCED"
}
Logic
Regulation
↓
Who is affected?
↓
What requirement changes?
↓
Cost / revenue / operation impact
↓
Affected companies
↓
Portfolio exposure

Misalnya:

New capital requirement
↓
Banking sector
↓
Potential impact on capital allocation
↓
User has 45% banking exposure
↓
Relevant
4. MACROECONOMIC

Ini bukan policy. Ini data/kejadian ekonomi.

Contoh:

“Inflasi Indonesia mencapai 3,2%, di atas ekspektasi 2,9%.”

Extraction
{
  "indicator": "CPI",
  "country": "Indonesia",
  "period": "August 2026",
  "actual": 3.2,
  "expected": 2.9,
  "previous": 2.8,
  "unit": "percent",
  "frequency": "monthly",
  "surprise": 0.3,
  "direction": "ABOVE_EXPECTATION"
}

Bagian expected vs actual sangat penting.

Karena:

Inflation = 3.2%

sendiri belum tentu menarik.

Yang lebih penting:

Actual 3.2%
Expected 2.9%
        ↓
Positive surprise +0.3%
Logic
Macro indicator
↓
Surprise
↓
Market interpretation
↓
Affected factors
↓
Sector/company exposure

Event extraction untuk financial news memang sering membutuhkan angka, tanggal, entity, dan atribut seperti intensity/modality.

7. GEOPOLITICAL

Contoh:

“Konflik di wilayah X meningkat setelah serangan terhadap fasilitas energi.”

Extraction
{
  "event_type": "CONFLICT_ESCALATION",
  "actors": [
    "Country A",
    "Country B"
  ],
  "location": "...",
  "action": "ATTACK",
  "target": "energy infrastructure",
  "severity": "HIGH",
  "status": "ESCALATING",
  "start_date": "...",
  "affected_commodities": [
    "oil"
  ],
  "affected_regions": [],
  "sanctions": false
}
Logic

Ini menarik karena bisa menghasilkan event chain:

Conflict escalation
        ↓
Supply disruption risk
        ↓
Oil ↑
        ↓
Energy companies potentially benefit
        ↓
Transportation costs ↑
        ↓
Airlines / logistics potentially pressured

Jadi jangan bikin:

War = bearish

Itu terlalu simplistik.

Porta harus memahami transmission mechanism.

8. MARKET

Ini berbeda dari event fundamental.

Market event adalah apa yang benar-benar terjadi di market.

Contoh:

“IHSG turun 3,2% dengan volume perdagangan 2x rata-rata.”

Extraction
{
  "instrument": "IDX Composite",
  "price_change_percent": -3.2,
  "volume": 25000000000,
  "average_volume": 12000000000,
  "volume_ratio": 2.08,
  "volatility": 0,
  "direction": "DOWN",
  "time": "...",
  "sector": null
}
Event types
MARKET
├── LARGE_PRICE_MOVE
├── VOLUME_SPIKE
├── VOLATILITY_SPIKE
├── MARKET_STRESS
└── SECTOR_ROTATION
Logic

Misalnya:

BBCA -5%
Volume 3x average
↓
Is it normal?
↓
No
↓
Check news/events
↓
Find possible catalyst

Ini bahkan bisa menjadi feedback loop ke Event Engine.

News Event
      ↓
Expected impact
      ↓
Actual market reaction
      ↓
MARKET EVENT

Jadi Porta bisa mengetahui:

“Kami mendeteksi event X, dan market kemudian bergerak Y.”

Ini bagian yang menurut gue paling penting buat desain Porta.

Setiap event sebaiknya melewati 5 layer:

1. DETECTION
      ↓
2. EXTRACTION
      ↓
3. NORMALIZATION
      ↓
4. IMPACT ANALYSIS
      ↓
5. PORTFOLIO RELEVANCE

Contohnya:

News

“BI raises rate 25 bps.”

1. Detection
category = MONETARY_POLICY
event_type = RATE_CHANGE
2. Extraction
previous_rate = 5.25
new_rate = 5.50
change = +25 bps
3. Normalization
central_bank_id = BI
country = ID
direction = INCREASE
4. Impact
rate ↑
↓
funding cost ↑
↓
interest-sensitive sectors
5. Portfolio relevance
User portfolio:
Banks 45%
Property 12%
Consumer 8%
Gold 5%

Financial sensitivity = HIGH
Property sensitivity = HIGH
Gold = LOW/MIXED

Lalu:

market_materiality = HIGH
portfolio_relevance = HIGH
confidence = 0.94
Jadi schema universalnya bisa seperti ini
